Sepotong Rasa

Branding · Illustration

My Role

  • Brand identity design
  • Mascot illustration

Context

Sepotong Rasa is a food brand that needed a visual identity.

Objective

Build the brand’s visual identity, centred on a set of mascot characters that carry its signature sandwich.

Process

Visual decisions visible in the final artwork:
  • A consistent orange and brown palette across every character, echoing bread and warm food.
  • Chef and barista uniforms with hats, aprons, and orange neckerchiefs as a repeating brand cue.
  • Ten male and female variations with different expressions and poses: smiling, winking, waving, serious, and holding a sandwich.
  • Bold black outlines in a comic style so the characters stay legible at small sizes.

Deliverables

  • A series of 10 mascot illustrations.
